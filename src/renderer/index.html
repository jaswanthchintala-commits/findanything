Welcome to FindAnything

FindAnything indexes the files on this computer only so you can search their names and entire contents instantly — just like the classic Google Desktop, rebuilt for modern Windows and macOS.

To do this, FindAnything needs your explicit permission to read the folders you choose. Nothing is ever uploaded: the index database, extracted text and all settings remain strictly on your local machine.

  • Deep-content indexing of TXT, MD, CSV, PDF, DOCX and XLSX files
  • You pick exactly which folders or drives are crawled
  • System folders are excluded by default and can be managed anytime

FindAnything will not scan anything until you grant permission. You can change this later from the Settings panel.
